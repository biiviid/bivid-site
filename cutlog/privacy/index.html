Privacy Policy

Last updated and effective 17 November 2026.

Who operates CutLog

Jerson Ombina operates CutLog. The app does not require an account.

What CutLog stores and why

CutLog stores the name you enter, birthday, height, weight, dates, daily targets, meals, portions, nutrition values and custom foods on your device for your log, charts and widgets. It uses your birthday to calculate age. Nutrition and weight records can reveal health-related information. CutLog does not use advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

Photos and other apps

If you take a photo for food estimation, CutLog can keep the image in its cache. You can choose an outside app to receive it. CutLog may copy a suggested prompt to your clipboard. CutLog does not send the photo to an AI service itself. The app you choose can process or upload the photo under its own privacy policy. You decide whether to paste the resulting estimate and save it in CutLog.

Export and Android backup

JSON exports contain meals and weigh-ins. Your document provider or sharing destination may store its own copy. Android backup is enabled. Depending on your device and settings, Android may copy app data, including nutrition records and profile information, to your backup account or transfer it to another device.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you remove them or clear CutLog’s app storage. You can delete meal entries and weigh-ins in the app. Clear the app cache to remove cached photos and manage exports, Android backups and copies sent to other apps separately.

Website and support

GitHub Pages hosts the website and logs visitor IP addresses for security. GitHub handles those records under the GitHub Privacy Statement. If you email support, we use your address and the information you send to respond. We keep support correspondence as needed to resolve your request, follow up on related issues, or meet legal obligations.

Your rights and contact

Depending on where you live and the law that applies, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data we hold about you. In the Philippines, you can also raise a concern with the National Privacy Commission. If you are a parent or guardian with a question about a child’s information, use the contact below. Contact Jerson Ombina at jersonombinabvd@gmail.com.
