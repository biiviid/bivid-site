support

for help with ping, write to bivid.ping@gmail.com. include your android version, your ping version, and the steps that led to the issue. share screenshots only after removing other people’s information and private task details.

install and update

  1. request a tester invitation at the address above.
  2. open the invitation on your android 10 or newer device and sign in with the invited email.
  3. open the ping 0.1.8 release in firebase app distribution and download the apk. follow android’s prompts for installing it from your download source.
  4. for updates, open the latest release in firebase app distribution and install it over your current copy. sync pending changes before uninstalling or clearing app storage.

sync and account access

first sign-in and group joining need a connection. if ping shows changes waiting to sync, reconnect and use retry. use review if a change could not be accepted. you can reset a password from the sign-in screen.

notifications and widgets

use ping settings to choose deadline alerts and android settings to allow notifications. android may delay background delivery. add a ping widget through your home-screen widget picker or ping settings.

privacy requests

email the address above or open account → delete account in ping for account closure. read the privacy notice.
