privacy notice

pilot notice · 13 november 2026

ping is operated by its creator, biiviid. contact bivid.ping@gmail.com for questions and privacy requests.

data used to run ping

ping uses your email address, sign-in identifier, display name, and username to provide your account and enable group invitations. it stores the tasks, reminders, groups, deadlines, outcomes and preferences you create. the app caches data and unsynced changes on your device so you can keep working offline. firebase authentication handles sign-in and account recovery. firebase stores and syncs your workspace, and its messaging service uses a device registration to deliver notifications and refresh shared data.

who can see shared work

signed-in users can look up public profile information such as display name and username. group members can read that group’s work under its membership rules. hosts can see individual handling or completion for items with tracking enabled. your personal space is for your account. android home screen widgets can display task titles and details to anyone who can see your device.

optional diagnostics

in settings, share crash reports enables firebase crashlytics on this device. reports can include technical identifiers, app and device details, and stack traces. ping adds no account id or task text to crash reports. reports may be stored on the device while uploads are off, and enabling reporting can send stored reports. switching reporting off takes effect after restarting the app. google provides firebase services and their related data handling. firebase privacy information.

retention and deletion

workspace data stays while you use the account. removed items have a 30-day recovery window. archived groups remain readable to eligible members for three calendar months. account closure ends access immediately, removes account identity and memberships through server cleanup, and leaves work already shared in groups owned by other people. personal task recovery records follow the 30-day retention window. minimal identifiers may be retained to prevent old requests from recreating deleted data.

your choices and requests

use ping to edit your profile, set notification and crash reporting preferences, or request account closure. transfer groups that still have other members before closing your account. email us to ask about access, correction, deletion, or other privacy rights. philippine national privacy commission data subject rights.
