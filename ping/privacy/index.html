privacy notice

Last updated and effective 17 November 2026.

Who operates Ping

Jerson Ombina, also known as biiviid, operates the Ping Android pilot.

Account, tasks and app data

Ping uses your email, account identifier, display name and username to provide sign-in and group invitations. It stores the tasks, reminders, groups, roles, deadlines, outcomes and preferences you create. It caches workspace information and unsynced changes on your device for offline use. Firebase Authentication handles sign-in and recovery. Google’s Firebase services store and sync workspace data, and Firebase Cloud Messaging uses a device registration for notifications and refresh requests. Firebase may also process technical information such as IP addresses as part of providing those services. See Firebase Privacy and Security.

Who can see shared work

Signed-in users can look up display names and usernames. Members can read group work within the permissions for that group. Hosts can see individual handling or completion when tracking is enabled. Android widgets and notifications can show task details to people who can see your device screen.

Optional crash reports

Crash reporting is off by default. You can enable Share crash reports in Settings. Firebase Crashlytics reports can contain technical identifiers, app and device details, stack traces and exception information. Enabling reporting may send reports stored on the device. Switching reporting off takes effect after restarting the app.

Retention and account deletion

We retain workspace data while you use the account. Removed items have a 30-day recovery window. Archived groups remain readable to eligible members for three calendar months. You can request account deletion in Settings. Closing an account ends access, and server cleanup removes account identity and memberships. Work shared in groups owned by other people may remain available to those groups. Minimal identifiers may be retained to prevent old requests from recreating deleted data. Firebase services can apply their own retention periods to service and diagnostic data.

Website and support

GitHub Pages hosts the website and logs visitor IP addresses for security. GitHub handles those records under the GitHub Privacy Statement. If you email support, we use your address and the information you send to respond. We keep support correspondence as needed to resolve your request, follow up on related issues, or meet legal obligations.

Your rights and contact

Depending on where you live and the law that applies, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data we hold about you. In the Philippines, you can also raise a concern with the National Privacy Commission. If you are a parent or guardian with a question about a child’s information, use the contact below.
